About Butlr

The hospitality
operating system.

Butlr connects the guest and the hotel on one live system. Travelers carry one app for every place they stay. Hotels run every department from one screen.

One system, three parts

The hotel side, the guest side, and the line between them.

The Portal

Your team’s command centre. Every department, request, stay and conversation, live on one screen.

The App

The whole hotel in the guest’s hand. They order, book, message the team and follow their bill.

The Thread

Both sides, one conversation. A tap on the guest’s phone shows on the right team’s screen the same second.

One app for travelers. One operating environment for hotels.

One Butlr Passport works at every hotel on Butlr, so a guest never signs up twice. Guests maintain a direct relationship with Butlr. Participating hotels receive authorized access to the information they need to serve them.

What is Butlr

The hospitality operating system.

One app for travelers. One operating environment for hotels. Butlr runs every department, request, stay and bill from one place, and puts the whole property in the guest’s hand.

One system, three parts

The hotel side, the guest side, and the line between them.

01 · The Portal

Your team’s command centre.

Where you build the entire hotel: dining, housekeeping, spa, the beach club, anything you run. Stand up a new department in an afternoon, then choose what your guests see.

For hotels
02 · The App

The whole hotel in the guest’s hand.

They open their stay, order, book, message the team and follow their bill without leaving their chair. Known by name, answered in a tap, from arrival to checkout.

For travelers
03 · The Thread

Both sides, one conversation.

A guest taps and it lands on the right team’s screen before the phone could have rung. The team accepts and the guest’s tracker moves. If it happens in your hotel, it shows on both screens.

For hotels

What changes when a hotel runs on Butlr.

01

Fewer phone calls

Requests arrive as taps, not calls, so the front desk is free to serve the guest in front of it.

02

Faster response

Every request is timed and lands on the team that does it, with no relay in between.

03

Real accountability

Every request owned, every step on record. Nothing dropped between shifts.

04

Happier guests

They see what is happening, in their language, and rate it in one tap. You see it live.

05

More direct revenue

Menus and services one tap away, direct bookings without commission, and missed orders made visible.

06

No project to start

No hardware and no integration. A browser on the devices you own, live within days.

Common questions

Frequently asked.

What is Butlr?+
Butlr is the luxury guest experience operating system for modern hospitality. One iOS app for guests, one live operational portal for venues — connected together as a single intelligent platform.
Who is Butlr for?+
Premium hospitality venues — hotels, resorts, beach clubs, branded residences, serviced apartments, members clubs, wellness resorts, marinas and similar. Anyone who takes guest experience seriously.
How do guests connect?+
Guests download the Butlr app for free. At check-in, they scan a QR code or enter a venue code — the app then knows their room, stay duration and preferences. No friction, no signup wall.
Does Butlr replace hotel staff?+
No. Butlr makes staff more effective. AI handles speed and routing; humans handle hospitality and recovery. The best luxury experiences are still delivered by people — Butlr just removes the friction in between.
Can we customize services?+
Yes. Every module (dining, spa, laundry, housekeeping, concierge, transport, etc.) is independently configurable. Add your own menu items, prices, response time targets and department routing. Elite and Enterprise tiers include custom branding.
Does Butlr support payments?+
Yes. Charge-to-room (folio) is supported on all tiers. Direct in-app payments, tipping and marketplace commissions are supported on Professional and above.
Can we manage staff?+
Yes. The Venue Portal includes staff accounts with roles (admin, manager, department staff), live presence, request assignment and per-staff performance tracking.
Can we upgrade packages later?+
Anytime. Upgrade or downgrade from the Venue Portal. Changes are pro-rated against your current billing cycle. New features unlock instantly.
Can Butlr support multiple venues?+
Yes. Multi-property management is included on Enterprise. Each property gets its own portal while sharing a unified analytics layer and centralized billing.
How does onboarding work?+
After your application is approved, our team walks you through profile setup, menu configuration, staff invitations and your first test guest. Most venues go live within 7 to 14 days. Elite and Enterprise include dedicated onboarding.

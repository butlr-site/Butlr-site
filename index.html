FAQ

Questions, honestly answered.

How long does it take to go live?

Most venues are live within 3–5 days. The application takes ~5 minutes, our review is typically within 48 hours, and once approved you can set up menus and invite your team the same day.

Do we need an engineering team?

No. Butlr is white-label and pre-built. You set up your venue through the web portal — no code, no integration with your PMS required.

What does the guest experience cost?

Free for the guest. Always. They download Butlr from the App Store and use it the moment they check into a partner venue.

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPayments for Kuwait and the GCC (KNET, KFAST, all GCC cards), Stripe for international cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay). Auto-routed by your venue's country.

What if a venue doesn't have a domain email or website?

Personal emails and small boutique venues are welcome — we just spend a little more time verifying. Your Google Maps listing, license and phone verification do most of the work.

How does data ownership work?

Guest data, menus, staff and orders belong to your venue. You can export anything, anytime. We never share your data with other venues.

Can a guest who's used Butlr at one hotel use it at mine?

Yes — that's the point. The guest's Butlr Passport (preferences, allergies, language) follows them to every venue on the network. The guest experience gets better the more places they stay.

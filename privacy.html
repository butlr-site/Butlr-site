Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026. This policy explains what information Butlr collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it short and in plain language because your privacy matters.

1. Who we are

Butlr ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Butlr Systems Establishment, a company registered in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Butlr operates worldwide and is available to guests staying at every venue that joins our network, in any country. We are the data controller for the personal information processed in connection with the Butlr mobile application ("App") on a global basis. Privacy questions: info@onebutlr.com.

Your venue is an independent controller of the guest data it receives, both data it collects outside Butlr and the profile data it views when you present your Butlr ID. Once a venue receives your data to serve you, it is responsible for handling it lawfully under its own privacy policy.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account and profile information. When you sign in (including via Sign in with Apple), we receive your name and a relay or real email address. You may also add a profile photo, nationality, gender, date of birth, phone number, country of residence and home address. Your name, photo, nationality, phone and email together form the Butlr profile card a venue sees when you check in (see section 4).
  • Identity documents. You may add your passport or ID number and upload a photo of the document inside the app so a venue can verify you at check-in. You control whether it is shared with a given venue and whether it is reused across the venues you visit, and you can remove it at any time.
  • Stay information. The venue, room number, check-in and check-out dates, length of stay, your loyalty tier, and any guest preferences you choose to enter (pillow, bed, room temperature, floor and view, breakfast time, morning drink, newspaper, and occasions such as a birthday or anniversary).
  • Allergies and special care. Allergies, dietary needs and special care are details about your health. You add them only if you want to. They are shared only with the venue hosting you so it can serve you safely, and you can switch sharing off or remove them at any time in the app.
  • Service requests. Orders and bookings you place (dining, spa, housekeeping, laundry, valet, errands), the time each one was placed and served, the amounts added to your bill, messages and photos you send to the venue team, ratings and feedback you give, and issues you report.
  • Valet. When you use valet, your car make, model, colour, plate number and ticket number, so the team can bring the right car.
  • Photos and camera. With your permission, the camera and photo library are used only to set a profile photo, upload an identity document, or send a photo to the venue team in a chat. We do not access your wider photo library.
  • Payment information. Butlr does not take payments in the app and never receives your card details. What you order during your stay is sent to the venue as line items on your room folio, and you settle your bill with the venue at the property.

2.2 Information we collect automatically

  • Device information. iOS version, device model, app version, language preference and time zone, to help us diagnose problems.
  • Location (optional). Read once, only when you press the SOS button, so the venue team can find you. It is never read in the background and the app works fully without it.
  • App usage. Crash reports linked to your account (Firebase Crashlytics) and app usage statistics (Firebase Analytics), so we can fix bugs and improve Butlr. You can turn usage data off in the app under Me, Privacy & data.

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your contacts, your photo library, calendar, health records (beyond the allergies and special care you choose to add), browser history, or location while you are away from a Butlr venue.
  • We do not sell your personal data. If Butlr Systems Establishment is sold, merged or transfers the service to another company, your account data transfers with it under this same policy, and you will be told.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • If you turn on app lock, Face ID and Touch ID are handled entirely by your device. Butlr never receives your biometric data.

3. How we use your information

  • Deliver the service: route your room-service order to the kitchen, your wake-up call to the front desk, your spa booking to the spa team.
  • Personalise your stay: your Butlr profile travels with you. Butlr keeps your preferences and your request and order history across every venue where you use Butlr, so the team at the next venue can serve you the way you like without asking twice. Venue staff see your preferences and habits, not the names of the other venues you stayed at. If you turn off Carry my Passport across venues, other venues do not see them.
  • Improve Butlr: diagnose bugs, measure feature usage in aggregate, and turn activity at Butlr venues into statistics and benchmarks in which you cannot be identified, to help venues improve their service.
  • Comply with the law: respond to lawful requests from authorities when required.

4. Sharing your information

  • Your venue, identity verification. When you give a venue your Butlr ID, or it is scanned at check-in, that venue's front desk can view your Butlr profile card: your name, profile photo, nationality, phone number, email address and its verification status, and how many stays you have had across the Butlr network. This lets the venue confirm it is really you before granting access to the app and your room, protecting you if your Butlr ID is ever lost or misused. Only a venue you present your Butlr ID to can see this; no venue can browse or search guests it has not checked in.
  • Your venue, identity documents. If you upload a passport or ID in the app, it is shared with the venue you are checking in to so they can verify you. You choose whether to reuse it at other venues, and you can delete it at any time.
  • Your venue, service and check-in records. When you stay at a Butlr partner venue, the venue receives the requests you make so they can fulfill them, and keeps records of your stay on its Butlr screens: your name, room, what you ordered, when, how quickly it was served and the amount billed. The venue's managers and owners can see reports built from these records, such as orders per day, service times and sales, which may list guest names and room numbers. These reports are used only to run and improve that venue's service. Any passport or other identity document you hand over at the desk is recorded by that venue as its own legal check-in record.
  • Apple. Sign in with Apple, push notifications and maps operate through Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Payments. Butlr does not process payments. Orders you place in the app are added to your room folio, and you settle your bill with the venue at the property, under its own payment arrangements. Butlr never receives or stores card numbers.
  • Service providers. Google Cloud (Firebase) for hosting and storage in the European Union, Vercel for the web portal and website, Sentry for error monitoring, Resend for email, Twilio for SMS, Anthropic for translating chat messages and answering guest questions in the venue chat, and Cloudflare for the bot check on the website booking form. Each is bound by data-processing terms and processes data only on our instructions. The current list, with what each one receives and where it runs, is at onebutlr.com/subprocessors.

5. Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), issued by Royal Decree M/19 and enforced by the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA), you have the right to know about the processing of your data, access it, request correction or completion, request destruction, object to processing that causes harm or violates PDPL, and withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. You may also lodge a complaint with SDAIA. Email info@onebutlr.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also delete your account in the App: Me, Delete my account.

6. Data retention

We retain your account, stay history, preferences, and request history for as long as you have an active Butlr account so that we can deliver the service across multiple stays. Your Butlr Passport preferences would be useless if we deleted them between visits. We also retain:

  • Identity documents you upload are readable by a venue only while it is hosting you. That access ends 24 hours after checkout and the document is cleared from our systems 30 days after checkout. You can delete it at any time in the app.
  • Stay records and folio history for the duration of your relationship with Butlr and our partner venues, to enable repeat-guest personalisation and loyalty benefits.
  • Aggregated, anonymised analytics indefinitely, to understand how Butlr is used and improve the service. This data cannot be linked back to you.
  • Financial records (orders, folios, invoices) for up to 10 years as required by applicable Saudi commercial, anti-money-laundering, tax and accounting laws, and the laws of other jurisdictions where Butlr operates or its partner venues are located.
  • Fraud-prevention records for up to 5 years where necessary to protect Butlr, our venues, and our guests from misuse.
  • Requests, orders and messages are part of the venue's records of your stay. They stay live for about 13 months and then move to a locked archive. Photos sent in chat are deleted after 90 days unless the venue sets another period. Support tickets are kept for up to 3 years.
  • Venue records. The records a venue keeps of the stays it hosts (orders, folio and check-in records) are part of that venue's property data. Butlr does not delete property data when a venue's agreement ends: it is archived and locked, and can be exported for the venue on request.

When you delete your account, we permanently remove your personally identifying information within 30 days, except for the categories above where retention is required by law or our legitimate business interests. After the retention period, data kept for those purposes is permanently deleted or fully anonymised.

7. Children

The Butlr app is for adults aged 18 and over. Children can be part of an adult’s stay, but cannot hold their own account, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 through the app. If you believe a child has created an account or given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers and global operation

Butlr is a globally available service. Our partner venues may be located in any country. The list of partner venues grows over time and is not limited to any single region.

Butlr is operated from the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Platform data is hosted on Google Cloud in the European Union (Belgium). Some service providers listed at onebutlr.com/subprocessors run in the United States and receive only the categories stated there, never identity documents. When you use Butlr, your data may also be processed in the country where your current Butlr partner venue is located, for the purpose of fulfilling your service requests. We rely on the safeguards permitted by the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and its Transfer Regulation, including data-processing terms with each provider, a documented transfer risk assessment, and encryption in transit and at rest.

9. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256). Card numbers never reach our servers. Identity documents you upload are encrypted and access-restricted so that only you and the venue currently hosting you can view them; every time a venue opens your document it is recorded, and you can see that record in the app. No other venue or guest can access them. Access by Butlr staff is limited to named administrators on a need-to-know basis. Our security measures are summarised at onebutlr.com/security.

No system is 100% secure. If a security breach creates a risk to your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority within 72 hours, as required by law.

10. Changes to this policy

Butlr is an evolving product. We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect new features (such as new services or expanded venue partnerships), to comply with new laws, or to reflect changes in how we operate. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

If we make material changes, meaning changes that meaningfully affect your rights or how we handle your data, we will show you the new version in the App, and email you where we have your address, and ask you to accept it before you continue using Butlr. If you do not agree to the changes, you may delete your account at any time.

Minor changes (such as clarifications, formatting, typo corrections, or updates to add new partner venues) take effect immediately without separate notice.

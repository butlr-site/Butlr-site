The problem

Luxury hospitality is still operated on tablets, printers, and duct tape.

i.

Hardware that breaks

In-room tablets get stolen, dropped, and replaced every 14 months. Printed menus are obsolete the day they're delivered. Five tablets per room at $400 each. Do the math.

ii.

Software that doesn't talk

One system for orders, another for housekeeping, a third for the spa, a fourth for folio, a fifth for chat. Your GM stitches reports together by hand at 11pm on Sundays.

iii.

Guests who forget you

The 7-figure couple in Suite 2104 last March? Their pillow preference, their breakfast time, their favorite cocktail — gone the moment they checked out. Next visit, you start from zero.

The solution

One guest app. One hotel portal. Synced live.

Your guest downloads Butlr once. The moment they enter your stay code, they see your hotel — your brand, your menu, your services. Every order they place, every message they send, lands instantly on your portal. At checkout, the entire stay saves to their personal Passport. Next time they visit any Butlr hotel, your name is in their bag.

How it works

Live for your guests in under five minutes.

You sign up

Visit butlr.app, fill in three fields, hit Activate. Your portal is live and your hotel is in the Butlr network. No sales calls. No IT project. No contracts.

Your team configures

Upload your logo, set your brand colors, add your menu, invite staff with PINs. Each department sees only what they need. Take twenty minutes if you want to do it right.

Your guests use it

Hand a guest their 4-digit stay code at check-in. They open Butlr, enter the code, and your hotel fills their screen. Every order routes to your team in real-time.

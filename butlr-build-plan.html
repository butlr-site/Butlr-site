Section VI · The honest risks

What can go wrong.

None of these are dealbreakers, but they're real. Better to see them today than at week 11.

App Store rejection
Apple sometimes rejects apps that look "thin." Mitigation: the app must work end-to-end with the pilot hotel before submission. Our spec already does. Expected outcome: approved on the second submission (1–2 weeks delay).
No pilot hotel
If you can't find one hotel to say yes, the app is hollow. Mitigation: start small — a boutique in your network, a friend of the family, a single hotel where you know the GM. Doesn't need to be Ritz. Needs to be real.
My limits on your laptop
I can write the code, but I can't physically be at your laptop. When something breaks in your environment (path issues, certs, builds), you'll need to paste me the error and we work through it. Plan for ~5–10 hours of friction across the 90 days.
Scope creep
You'll want to add Whisper, Tip Jar, Bring-It, Passport, the Apple Wallet key. Resist. Phase 1 is the minimum viable butler. Everything else is Phase 2 with real engineers.
You burn out
10 hours a week for 12 weeks is real work alongside a job. Mitigation: stick to the weekly plan. Skip a week if needed — the plan flexes. Don't try to do two weeks in one.

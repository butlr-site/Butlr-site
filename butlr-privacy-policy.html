Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 May 2026. This policy explains what information Butlr collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep this short and in plain language because your privacy matters.

1. Who we are

Butlr ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Forty Eight Hours Company, headquartered in Kuwait City, State of Kuwait. We are the data controller for the personal information processed in connection with the Butlr mobile application ("App"). Privacy questions: hi@bader.me.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account information. When you sign in (including via Sign in with Apple), we receive your name and a relay or real email address.
  • Stay information. The hotel, room number, and any guest preferences you choose to enter (pillow type, breakfast time, allergies, dietary requirements).
  • Service requests. Orders you place (dining, spa, housekeeping, errands), messages you send to the Concierge, voice transcripts you create with Whisper.
  • Payment information. When you pay via Apple Pay, we receive a single-use payment token from Apple — we never see your card number. If you choose "Charge to room", we send only the line items to the hotel; the hotel handles your card.

2.2 Information we collect automatically

  • Device information. iOS version, device model, app version, language preference, time zone — to help us diagnose problems.
  • Approximate location. Only when you grant permission and only to detect which Butlr hotel you are at. We do not track you outside the hotel.
  • App usage. Crash reports and anonymous diagnostics so we can fix bugs.

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, calendar, health data, browser history, or location while you are away from a Butlr hotel.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

3. How we use your information

  • Deliver the service: route your room-service order to the kitchen, your wake-up call to the front desk, your spa booking to the spa team.
  • Personalise your stay: pre-load your Butlr Passport preferences at any hotel you check in to.
  • Improve Butlr: diagnose bugs, measure feature usage in aggregate.
  • Comply with the law: respond to lawful requests from authorities when required.

4. Sharing your information

  • Your hotel. When you stay at a Butlr partner hotel, the hotel receives the requests you make so they can fulfill them.
  • Apple. Sign in with Apple, Apple Pay, push notifications, and the Speech framework operate through Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Payment processors. When you pay via Apple Pay, your payment token is processed by our payment provider (Stripe, Inc.) under their privacy policy.
  • Service providers. Hosting (AWS), error tracking (Sentry), product analytics (PostHog). All bound by data-processing agreements and process data only on our instructions.

5. Voice (Whisper) and microphone

The Whisper feature uses the iOS on-device Speech framework to convert your voice to text. The audio is processed by Apple under their Speech framework terms. We receive only the resulting text transcript — we do not store the raw audio.

6. Your rights

Under the Kuwait Data Privacy Protection Regulation, GDPR (EEA), the UK Data Protection Act, CCPA (California), and similar laws in the GCC and other regions, you have the right to access, correct, delete, export, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Email hi@bader.me and we will respond within 30 days. You can also delete your account in the App: Me tab → Sign out → Delete account.

7. Data retention

We retain your account and stay history for as long as you have an active Butlr account. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to keep it for tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes (typically up to 7 years for financial records).

8. Children

Butlr is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Butlr is operated from the State of Kuwait, with infrastructure in the EU, US, and GCC. When you use Butlr from another country, your data is transferred to and processed in those countries. We use standard contractual clauses to ensure adequate protection.

10. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Payment data is never stored on our servers — we use tokenized payments via Apple Pay and Stripe. We limit employee access to personal data on a strict need-to-know basis.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will notify you in the App and by email at least 30 days before the changes take effect.

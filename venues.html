Why Butlr exists

Every system in a hotel runs a department. None runs the guest.

The PMS runs the rooms, the POS the payments, the lock the door. The part a guest actually feels, the service itself, never had a system. Butlr is that system, and it connects every department that serves them.

Without Butlr

One dinner, and everyone in between.

  1. 9:12She calls the front desk. It rings, and rings.
  2. 9:14Someone answers. She orders dinner to the room.
  3. 9:15The desk relays the order to the kitchen, and the kitchen confirms with the desk.
  4. 9:45She waits with no word. Is it even coming?
  5. 9:50Late. She calls the desk again to check.

A chain of hand-offs, two calls, and a guest kept in the dark.

With Butlr

The same dinner, straight through.

  1. 9:12She opens the app and orders dinner. One tap.
  2. 9:12It lands on the kitchen’s screen, with no desk in between.
  3. 9:13The kitchen accepts. She watches it on her phone: preparing.
  4. 9:31On its way. A knock, right on time.

No calls, no relay, no waiting blind. Now picture every department in the hotel working the same way.

Questions

What GMs ask first.

Do we need a PMS?

No. Butlr operates independently for its supported workflows: stays, check-in by Butlr ID, departments, orders, chat and the bill.

Do we need new hardware?

No. The portal runs in a browser on the phones, tablets and computers you already have. A tablet at the kitchen pass or the spa desk can sign in as that department.

How long does it take to go live?

Core departments go live the same day we finish setup with your team, and the full property within days. There is no integration project.

Which languages?

The portal and the app are in Arabic and English, and guest messages are translated both ways so each side reads its own language.

How do guests start using it?

They download Butlr from the App Store and register in seconds. At check-in, the desk links their Butlr ID to the stay and the whole hotel opens on their phone.

How is Butlr priced?

A subscription per room per month, in your currency, with no hardware or setup project to pay for. Ask us for the figure for your property.

What happens to our records?

Every order, stay and bill created at your hotel stays in your hotel’s history in the portal, with a full audit log.
